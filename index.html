About the Community

A lineage rooted in memory, migration, and kinship.

Iraqi Biradari, العراقي برادری, or Iraqi Tamimi is a Sunni Muslim community in India. Community history connects the Biradari with Bani Tamim, migration through Sindh, and later settlement in Eastern Uttar Pradesh.

The Biradari preserves identity through family Shajra, community records, oral histories, events, and shared publications. This platform reorganizes the older GoDaddy content into a cleaner heritage archive rather than copying its structure directly.

The genealogy explorer remains isolated from the content system so lineage research can evolve independently and safely.
