About the Community

A lineage rooted in memory, migration, and kinship.

Iraqi Biradari, العراقي برادری, also referred to as Iraqi Shaikh, is described as a Sunni Muslim community in India, chiefly associated with eastern Uttar Pradesh districts including Ghazipur, Azamgarh, Ballia, Deoria, and Gorakhpur.

Community history connects the Biradari with immigrants from Iraq and later settlement in Ghazipur during the Delhi Sultanate period. The community preserves identity through family Shajra, oral histories, documents, public programs, and shared publications.

Summary adapted from “Iraqi Biradari,” Wikipedia.
