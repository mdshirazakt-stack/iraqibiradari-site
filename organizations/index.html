Organizations

Community organizations working for the Iraqi Biradari.

Trusts, NGOs, and individuals doing meaningful work for the community can have their own space here — post their work, events, appeals, and connect with those who need support.

How It Works

One umbrella, many efforts.

Any organization — a registered trust, an NGO, or an individual working in education, welfare, or community service — can apply for a space under this platform. Each gets their own page, can post their own events, and can receive donor support and beneficiary registrations.

Charitable Trusts

Welfare & Relief

Trusts running relief, education sponsorships, or marriage assistance programmes for the community.

NGOs & Associations

Registered Bodies

Formally registered organizations doing heritage preservation, community development, or advocacy work.

Individuals

Personal Initiatives

Community members running education drives, scholarship funds, or welfare efforts in a personal capacity.
