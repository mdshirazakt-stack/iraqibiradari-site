Matrimony Service Policy

Code of Conduct · Disclaimer · Limitation of Liability · Community Welfare

Version May2026-v1 · Effective immediately

Purpose of the Service

The matrimony section of this platform is provided as a community welfare initiative to help eligible individuals and families connect with one another for marriage-related discussions and introductions.

The platform acts solely as a facilitator and communication medium. It does not function as a marriage bureau, matchmaking agency, investigative agency, legal advisor, or guarantor of any individual, family, profile, claim, or relationship.

Accuracy of Information

By submitting a profile, users agree that:

  • All information provided is true, accurate, and complete.
  • No material facts have been intentionally hidden.
  • Information relating to age, marital status, education, occupation, income, health condition, family details, and personal background must be disclosed honestly.
  • Users are responsible for updating their information if circumstances change.

Providing false, misleading, incomplete, or fraudulent information may result in:

  • Immediate removal of the profile.
  • Permanent suspension from the platform.
  • Reporting to community authorities where appropriate.
  • Legal action where applicable under law.

Independent Verification Required

All users, families, and guardians are strongly advised to independently verify:

  • Identity and family background
  • Education and employment
  • Income claims and character references
  • Health information and marital status
  • Any other information deemed relevant before proceeding

The platform does not verify every claim made by users and does not guarantee the accuracy of submitted information.

Code of Conduct

Users shall not:

  • Submit fake or misleading profiles.
  • Use the service for harassment or stalking.
  • Misrepresent their identity.
  • Use another person's photographs or information without permission.
  • Solicit money, loans, investments, gifts, or financial assistance.
  • Engage in abusive, threatening, defamatory, or inappropriate behavior.
  • Use the platform for commercial purposes without permission.
  • Collect personal information for unauthorized use.

Violation may result in immediate removal without notice.

Ghosting and Serious Intent Policy

This platform is intended for genuine matrimonial purposes only. Users are expected to:

  • Communicate respectfully.
  • Respond within a reasonable timeframe where possible.
  • Avoid creating profiles without genuine intent to pursue matrimonial discussions.
  • Avoid repeatedly initiating discussions and then disappearing without explanation.

While occasional non-response is understandable, repeated misuse or bad-faith participation may result in restrictions or removal from the service. The platform reserves the right to remove profiles that appear inactive, non-serious, fraudulent, or disruptive to the community.

Privacy and Confidentiality

The platform is committed to maintaining the highest reasonable level of privacy for users. Administrators will make reasonable efforts to:

  • Protect submitted personal information.
  • Limit unnecessary disclosure of sensitive details.
  • Restrict access to profile information as appropriate.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute privacy or security. Users should exercise discretion before sharing personal, financial, or sensitive information with other individuals.

No Matchmaking Guarantee

The platform does not guarantee suitable matches, marriage outcomes, compatibility, relationship success, family acceptance, engagement, or marriage. Any interaction, communication, meeting, engagement, or marriage resulting from introductions made through this platform is solely between the concerned individuals and families.

Limitation of Liability

The website owners, administrators, volunteers, moderators, trustees, contributors, and webmasters shall not be responsible or liable for:

  • Incorrect or misleading information submitted by users.
  • Misrepresentation by individuals or families.
  • Failed matrimonial discussions, rejected proposals, or ghosting.
  • Emotional distress arising from interactions.
  • Financial loss or fraudulent conduct by users.
  • Identity misrepresentation or family disputes.
  • Engagement disputes, marital disputes, separation, or divorce.
  • Criminal conduct by any user.
  • Any direct, indirect, incidental, consequential, or special damages arising from use of the service.

Users participate entirely at their own discretion and risk.

Community Welfare Disclaimer

This service is operated solely as a community welfare initiative intended to help members connect on a common platform. The administrators and webmaster act only as facilitators and do not endorse, certify, investigate, guarantee, or recommend any person, family, profile, proposal, or marriage.

All decisions regarding communication, meetings, engagement, marriage, and related matters remain the sole responsibility of the individuals and families involved.

By using this service, users acknowledge and agree to these terms and release the platform, its administrators, volunteers, trustees, and webmaster from any claims, liabilities, disputes, losses, or damages arising from the use of the matrimony service.

"I confirm that all information submitted by me is true and accurate to the best of my knowledge. I understand that this platform acts only as a facilitator and I remain solely responsible for independently verifying all matrimonial information and proposals."
